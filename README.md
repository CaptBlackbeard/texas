# Texas, Brisket First

A barbecue weekend plan for Austin or Dallas, Thursday 10 to Sunday 13 December 2026, for two adults and a small child.

**Live site:** https://captblackbeard.github.io/texas/
**Map:** https://captblackbeard.github.io/texas/map/

## What's on the site

- An Austin / Dallas switch: each city has its own four-day itinerary with barbecue for lunch and dinner
- Barbecue joints with what to order, lines, sell-out times and beer, plus a Lockhart day trip
- Marriott-family hotels with walking counts
- Children's museums (Thinkery, Perot Museum) and December light shows
- The Warhammer Citadel in Grapevine
- Coffee and family-friendly breweries
- Nonstop flights from Burbank, with fares for both cities
- Interactive map with walking circles around each hotel

## Notes

- The site is static HTML served by GitHub Pages; there is no build step in this repo.
- Pages are marked `noindex`, so search engines are asked not to list them.
- Photo credits and every source are listed at the bottom of the page.
- Music: *Sallie Johnson* and *Billy in the Low Ground*, traditional fiddle tunes played by Texas fiddler Eck Robertson (Victor, 1922). Public domain in the US, via Wikimedia Commons.
